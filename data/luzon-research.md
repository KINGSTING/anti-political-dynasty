# PULITIKLAN — Luzon evidence and coverage audit

Reviewed 2026-09-16. The map includes **38 provinces, 17 Metro Manila localities and eight independent cities outside NCR**. 356 networks and 1157 people were added beyond NCR. Across the atlas there are 358 family groups and 1217 distinct family profiles. 62 of 63 mapped places have a documented network; geographic coverage is wider than relationship coverage.

## What this release establishes

A displayed line is a sourced relationship, not an accusation or a finding of unlawful conduct. A darker marker counts the relationships documented in this dataset, not the total size, wealth, electoral strength or control of a family. Provincial and city circles cannot be fairly ranked as equally researched units. Green badges identify current officeholders; other living profiles have no status badge. Deceased is a separate life-status cue. Titles distinguish national and local office. The office-status review replaces dated classifications using current-term directories and later records, while retaining historical sources.

This is not an exhaustive roster of Luzon officials. Municipal councils, barangays, in-law networks and less prominent families need further coverage. Shared surnames, party alliances and joint photographs do not establish kinship. Unretrieved sources and unresolved image identities are not treated as evidence. Corrections can be submitted from each profile or relationship.

- Ram Bautista is the Cavite vice governor profiled here; aliases are not separate people. Cherry/Czarina and Dax/Dakila likewise have one identity. Emmeline Aglipay-Villar keeps her existing NCR identity in the Cagayan network.
- Adoption is labeled explicitly as an adoptive relationship and is not encoded as a blood relationship. [PCIJ · Bicol family election outcomes](https://pcij.org/2025/05/22/neophyte-shakes-up-catanduanes-politics-amid-familiar-wins-in-bicol/)
- Families that lost seats in 2025 retain historical profiles. The map does not imply that their former offices remain under their control.
